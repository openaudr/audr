# Implementations

Everything that produces, consumes or operates on [AUDR](spec/SPEC.md) records, in three
categories.

| Category | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Adapters | Produce records from a harness, router or other agent runtime. [What an adapter is](adapters/README.md). |
| Sinks | Consume records and deliver them to a destination. [What a sink is](sinks/README.md). |
| Tools | Read or write records outside an adapter or sink: analyzers, validators, converters, dashboards. |
